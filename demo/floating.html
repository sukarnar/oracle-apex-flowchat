The same loan form with the assistant in floating mode: a launcher in the corner, a peek bar when minimized, and a panel when open.

Loan application (existing form)

  • Try jane.doe@example.com to see the customer lookup prefill the name.
  • Try x@mailinator.com to see a server-side validator reject it.
  • Ask for more than 100k to branch into the affordability check.
  • At the summary, click Edit on the amount to see the flow re-branch.
